Privacy Policy for TidyShare

Effective Date: November 2026

TidyShare is developed and provided as a personal productivity tool to organize photos and scanned documents into folders and transfer them directly to a user's local PC (via SMB over Wi-Fi) or personal Google Drive account.

1. Data Collection and Storage

TidyShare does not operate proprietary cloud servers and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal files, photos, videos, or documents on third-party servers. All folders, settings, and files remain locally on your device and are transmitted exclusively to the destinations configured by you (your local network SMB share and/or your personal Google Drive).

2. Permissions Used

  • Camera & Photo Library / Storage: Used solely to capture photos, scan documents, create PDFs, and select existing media to organize into folders.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi: Used solely to connect to shared folders on your PC over your local network (SMB).
  • Google Sign-In / Google Drive API: Used solely when you explicitly connect your Google account to upload files to your own Google Drive and back up/restore your folder configuration.

3. Third-Party Services

If you choose to enable Google Drive sync, authentication and file uploads are handled directly through official Google APIs under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data at all times. You can delete local files, folders, or revoke Google Drive access directly within the app settings or through your Google Account permissions.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: cacciolaluigi@gmail.com (or your support email listed on Google Play).
